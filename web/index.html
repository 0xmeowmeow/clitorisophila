Inside the work

The nerve branches and regional sensitivities are imagined. Touch enters the full MaleCNS fly graph through synthetic bindings. Light follows computed activity.

Hearts follow computed reward-cell activity. No experience is measured. The live model downloads about 76 MB and runs in your browser.
